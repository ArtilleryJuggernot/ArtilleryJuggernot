## 🧭 About me

I'm **Hugo Jacquel**, a French software engineer and the founder of **[Atomixia](https://atomixia.fr)** — a digital-engineering studio building **AI-agentic solutions, custom SaaS, web & mobile applications and business automation** for companies, associations and institutions.

I design systems end to end: from the data model and the API to the UI, the CI/CD pipeline, the security posture and the growth strategy. My sweet spot is the **Forward-Deployed-Engineer** mindset — landing on a messy real-world problem, shipping a working product fast, then hardening it.

- 🚀 **Founder & Director — [Atomixia](https://atomixia.fr)** · custom SaaS, AI agents, automation, web & mobile, cybersecurity consulting *(since 2024)*
- 🎓 **Final-year engineering student** — *ICS major (Computer Science & Cybersecurity)* at **CPE Lyon**
- 🧑‍💻 **Apprentice software & web developer @ G2S (Groupama)** — AI automation & cybersecurity *(since Sept. 2025)*
- 🤖 I build **agentic AI systems**: LLM orchestration, RAG pipelines, MLOps, workflow automation
- 🔐 **Cybersecurity** is a second home: pentest, DevSecOps, offensive-security tooling, network & low-level
- 🌍 Open to opportunities abroad — Singapore, Dubai / UAE, US (Bay Area), Asia
- ⚡ Motto: *"high and low level magician"* — I'm as comfortable in a kernel driver as in a React dashboard

> **📌 A note on my repositories — most of them are private.**
> The large majority of my work lives in **private repositories**, because it belongs to my company **Atomixia** and to my clients, or is protected by **confidentiality / NDA agreements**. This profile therefore highlights a curated selection of what can be shown publicly, plus the full list of what I'm working on. Feel free to reach out on **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hugo-jacquel)** for a private walkthrough.

## 🏆 Flagship projects

> A curated selection. Everything below is **built, maintained and shipped** by me — most repositories are private (company / client confidentiality).

### 🧠 Zeus — collaborative knowledge & project OS
**`PHP · Laravel · Livewire · Flutter · MySQL`** — *private*
> The modern "second brain": an all-in-one **SaaS for note-taking, project management and self-organisation**. Visual, collaborative, offline-friendly, with a companion **Flutter mobile app** (`zeus_app`) sharing a clean-architecture back-end.
> → Note graphs, projects, tasks, resources, self-organisation model, real-time collaboration.

### 🏗️ Artemisia — public-works & construction management
**`Laravel · Blade · MySQL`** — *private*
> A web solution for **roadworks / public-works site management and accounting**: worksites, teams, materials, budgets and reporting for the BTP sector.

### 🦺 SecurityBTP / SecurityBTP-SaaS — BTP compliance platform
**`Laravel 11 · Livewire 3 · Tailwind · Flutter`** — *private*
> SaaS for **EPI (personal protective equipment) & DUERP management** in the construction industry — the regulatory compliance layer companies actually need, with a mobile inspection app.

### 📇 ContactVy — CRM for BTP companies
**`Laravel 11 · Livewire · Alpine.js · Three.js`** — *private*
> A **full CRM** for construction businesses: contacts, worksites, quotes and follow-up — with an immersive Three.js UI.

### 🗺️ ProspectionMAP — advanced prospecting map
**`Next.js · TypeScript · Leaflet / OpenStreetMap`** — *private*
> An interactive **prospecting map** for Lyon & Villeurbanne (companies, schools, associations) built on French **open data**, with server-side clustering and a unified structure profile. The engine behind my lead-generation workflows.

### 🛰️ Nora — AI agent fleet control plane
**`TypeScript · Docker · Kubernetes · Apache 2.0`** — *private*
> A self-hosted, runtime-neutral **control plane for OpenClaw & Hermes agents** — deploy, monitor and operate **fleets of AI agents** on infrastructure you control.

### 🏛️ Atomixia Bastion — multi-agent orchestrator
**`Next.js 15 · Prisma · MariaDB · Qdrant · Redis`** — *private*
> A multi-agent control plane to orchestrate **Hermes** instances (NASA-style hub pattern) with a central ACP Hub, a **global knowledge base (Qdrant)** and a Universe → Project → Mission → Task hierarchy.

### 🔎 LangGraph RAG — conversational agent for Jira & Confluence
**`Python · LangGraph · RAG`** — *private*
> Production-ready **LangGraph RAG** system querying Jira issues and Confluence docs, with typed state management, AI intent detection and conditional routing.

### 🧑‍✈️ Augmented Project Manager — humans + AI agents
**`Python · AI agents`** — *private*
> Hybrid project-management app integrating Jira user stories, backlog, team, goals and governance — turning each story into agent-executable Gherkin criteria while keeping the **human in the loop**.

### 🐛 Bug-Bounty Agents — offensive-security AI prompts
**`Shell · LLM agents · MIT`** — *private*
> A curated arsenal of **specialised AI-agent prompts for bug bounty, pentest and offensive-security** workflows — drop-in personas for any agent-capable LLM.

### 🎓 CPE-Alternance — internship & apprenticeship platform
**`Next.js · Docker · Prisma`** — *private*
> A platform for CPE Lyon students, recruiters and faculty, importing **5,815 historical internships** on first boot and shipping with a built-in **Hermes gateway**.
